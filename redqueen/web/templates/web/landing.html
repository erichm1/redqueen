The pipeline

Five steps, one accountable record

  1. 1

    Intake

    Upload a photo or video with its time and place. Every face is detected and boxed.

  2. 2

    Identify

    Each face is compared with registered portraits and scored by similarity against a threshold.

  3. 3

    Profile

    A suspect profile gathers the match evidence, the person's record and any other appearances.

  4. 4

    Validate

    An officer reviews the evidence. A judge applies the judgment and, optionally, a sentence.

  5. 5

    Foresee

    Three precogs vote on future risk, with any minority report kept in plain sight.

Capabilities

Everything a case needs, in one workspace

COMPSTAT dashboard

Incidents, hot spots, deployment split and a high-risk watchlist over 7, 28, 90 or 365 days.

Incident records

Date, time, precinct, place and map link, with every individual involved and their related occurrences.

Match evidence

The intake face cropped beside the registered photo, with similarity, threshold and the other candidates.

Webcam enrolment

Enrol a face straight from the camera and attach a record, with erasure on request.

Sentence guidance

Recommendations from the record: fines, service hours, probation or prison, and pending or defaulted penalties.

People registry

Search people and open their record, photos, risk and every profile they appear in.

Safeguards

A system that stays answerable

Humans decide

A match is only a lead. Profiles are validated by a person, and risk scores are advisory.

Closed access

There is no public sign-up. Accounts are issued by an administrator, and permissions follow the role.

Private media

Faces and footage are served only to signed-in users, never from a public address.

Everything is logged

Every action lands in the audit trail, readable by those with the right permission.

Authorised personnel only.

Sign in with the account your administrator created for you. Access is logged.
