# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Reverted the 3D portal room (the Red Queen beside the gate, 3D "ACCESSING" lettering, name plate and lava progress bar) on request: the portal is back to the 3D gate with the flat message and progress bar.
  `buildQueen()` stays exported from `queen3d.js` (the logo scene uses it); the text/font add-ons were removed again.
- The RED QUEEN wordmark is now full blood red with a breathing glow: both words on the sign-in banner (RED was white), the top-bar name next to the badge, and the flat `banner.svg`
  (`--blood: #cf0f1f`; the glow animation stops for reduced motion but the colour stays).
- The bone spikes and horns were removed from the portal gate on request (the ring, glowing cracks, runes and gem stay).
- The hellgate portal is back to its first design at the user's request: a full-screen fire-vortex fragment shader (three.js) behind the SVG stone gate, which grows to fill the screen in the last
  0.9 s before the app opens. The 3D-scene version (and the version without the ending) were dropped. The CSS-only gate remains the no-WebGL fallback.
- New `redqueen/web/static/web/glkit.js` holds the helpers used by the logo character (`taperedTube`, `canvasTexture`, `seeded`).
- Hellgate portal after sign-in: a successful login now goes to `/portal/` for `PORTAL_DELAY_SECONDS` (5) with an "Accessing..." message and progress bar, then opens the home page or the
  originally requested page. Fire-vortex fragment shader (three.js) inside an SVG stone gate (glowing cracks, rotating runes, generated by the `gate_ornaments` tag); CSS-only fallback without WebGL. `next` is validated (same-site only, never the portal itself). Bad sign-ins never show it.
- Privacy eyes: on the sign-in page she closes her eyes while the password field has focus and opens them when it loses focus. The 3D model has real eyelids
  (skin shells revealed by a clipping plane, eased open/close, a lash line when shut) plus an idle blink; the 2D fallback has lids driven by the same `eyes-closed` class.
  Reduced-motion users get the state change without easing. `queen.setEyesClosed(closed, immediate)` is the public switch.
- Wings are veined: a procedural membrane texture (colour, bump and glowing-vein maps with branching capillaries), bone-thick veins, secondary struts and claws; the 2D fallback got veins too.
- Fixed the scepter crossing between her legs: the floor circle now lies in front of her feet and she leans forward from the hips to reach it (the bodice moved into the leaning upper body so it stays attached).
- A blood-red RQ (same font stack as the wordmark) is written inside the circle, revealed by a wedge that follows the scepter tip on the same 3 s lap clock (also in the 2D fallback).
- New `web/test_scene3d.py`: geometric checks (scepter vs legs, tip vs feet, arm reach) plus content checks; camera looks down slightly so the circle reads.
- Logo character changes: solid red eyes, light blue skin, larger wings, a fully red two-tone dress, and the punch was replaced by a scepter held in the lead hand whose tip
  sweeps a glowing circle on the floor (the elbow is solved by two-bone IK; the circle is the tip's recent path, fading with age). The 2D fallback art, the badge, favicon
  and `queen.glb` were regenerated to match.
- Logo is now a real 3D model (three.js, procedural, PBR materials, soft shadows) in a fighting stance with a jab every 6 s, live on the sign-in page and following the mouse;
  falls back to the 2D art without WebGL and shows a still frame for reduced motion. Top-bar badge and favicon are renders of the model (`logo.png`, `favicon.png`).
  three.js r170 (MIT) vendored locally; `web/art/render_logo.py` regenerates the renders and exports `web/art/queen.glb` for Blender.
- Removed on request: the FIGHT! splash, the health bars and the VS label (and their animations) from the sign-in scene.
- (superseded by the 3D logo above) Logo animated as an arcade fighting-game character: redrawn as a devil-queen fighter in a martial-arts guard stance (wide knee-bent stance, fists up, tail, wings)
  and built as a jointed rig. Idle: knee flex + torso bob, swaying arms/dress/tail/hair, flapping wings, pulsing eyes; every 6 s a jab with a hit spark.
  Sign-in page: mouse-driven 3D tilt (layers inline SVG on one animation clock).
  Reduced-motion users get a still image. Tests check keyframes/classes match the artwork and that the committed files match `web/art/build_queen.py`.
- Intakes are now full incident records: `captured_at` (editable capture time, separate from submission time), `location`, `latitude`/`longitude`
  next to `precinct`, and `related_occurrences` (every infraction of the identified people, linked when the intake is processed). Infractions gain a `location`.
  Migrations `registry/0006` and `cases/0004-0006` (existing intakes are backfilled: capture time = submission time, related occurrences derived from their matched faces).
- Intake page: Incident record (date, weekday, time, hour, place, coordinates + map link), Individuals involved, Occurrences related. The list shows capture time, hour,
  location and counts and sorts by capture time; profile/person record tables show time and location. Form: capture time (defaults to now), place, coordinates, "use my location".
- API: `captured_at`, `date`, `time`, `hour`, `location`, coordinates, `individuals`, `occurrences`; validation for future times and half-given coordinates.
- `REDQUEEN_TIME_ZONE` sets the zone for displayed dates and hours (default UTC). Dummy occurrences now have places and varied times of day.
- My profile (`/account/`) for the signed-in user, following the structure and fields of callum_freight_hub's user account screen (which has no
  self-service profile page of its own): Account card, Groups, Permissions, About access levels, plus RedQueen capabilities and personal activity.
  Name and email are editable and audited; username, active/staff/superuser, groups and permissions are read-only so nobody can grant themselves access.
  Themed password change pages (routed explicitly because the admin app ships same-named templates).
- Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-ins are now written to the audit log (attempted username only, never the password).
- Logo redrawn as a devil queen with 3D shading (horns, pointed ears, slit-pupil eyes, fang, bat wings, trident-and-heart scepter). Layered art stacked
  in CSS 3D on the sign-in page with mouse-tilt parallax; generator script `web/art/build_queen.py`. A custom `banner.jpg/png/webp` still overrides it.
- New logo: an original female fantasy-RPG character in a red gown (tiara, heart-topped scepter) as SVG. Round badge in the top bar and as favicon,
  full figure in `queen.svg`, and the sign-in banner. A test checks the SVGs are valid with no dangling gradient references.
- Enrol page rebuilt in the same layout and with the same fields as the profile's Subject card (Name, Person ID, Date of birth, Age range,
  Gender, Occurrences, Registered, Face templates): live photo preview, age range derived from the date of birth (or chosen when unknown),
  record preview per scenario with the resulting occurrence count. A test keeps the two pages' field lists identical.
- Full profile page: registered photos, side-by-side match evidence (intake face crop vs registered photo with similarity %), record summary,
  live complete record (frozen copy kept), other appearances/profiles, audit trail. Same subject card and summary on the person page.
- Enrolment now stores a cropped face image per template (`FaceTemplate.photo`, migration `registry/0005`; `STORE_ENROLMENT_PHOTOS` turns it off).
  Photos and intake media are served only to logged-in users and their files are deleted whenever the person/intake is deleted by any route.
  Seeded dummy people get their portraits as registered photos. Templates enrolled before this change show a placeholder.
- Merged the standalone `precog` project into redqueen. Its `Person` entity is now part of `registry.Person`:
  `uuid` (unique, public id), `age_range` (derived from date of birth, or set by hand), `gender`, `total_occurrences`
  (auto-maintained from infractions). Migrations `registry/0002-0004` add the fields, backfill existing rows (unique UUIDs,
  age ranges, counts) and then enforce uniqueness; verified on a populated database.
- New `/api/persons/` (list, create, update, delete) addressed by UUID with `q` / `age_range` filters, `/api/persons/{uuid}/risk/`;
  the numeric `/api/persons/{id}/risk/` route still works. Deleting via the API erases face data and media like the web action.
- Person list/detail/enrolment/admin show age range, gender and occurrences. `__str__` added to every entity.
- Entity health tests (`redqueen/test_entities.py`): every model has rows from a full pipeline run, opens in the admin,
  passes validation, and matches its migrations. A test asserts age/gender never change the risk score.
- Django apps: `registry` (people, faces, infractions, penalties), `vision` (OpenCV YuNet/SFace
  recognition, photo + video), `cases` (intake, suspect profile, validation, judgment, audit trail),
  `precog` (three-voter risk model with minority report, COMPSTAT report), `api` (DRF + Swagger).
- `seed_dummy_data`, `redqueen_demo` and `fetch_models` management commands.
- Dummy data: John, Jane, Susan and Mark Doe plus background records.
- `web` frontend: COMPSTAT dashboard, intake upload, profile review/judgment, people, audit log; login required, permission-gated actions, uploaded media only served to authenticated users.
- `seed_dummy_data --users` creates dev logins `officer` / `judge`.
- Webcam capture (photo or 5 s video) on the intake page; webcam enrolment page with dummy record scenarios (`registry.demo`); erase-person action that also removes matching intake media. Enrolment stores face templates only, rejects photos without exactly one face and faces already enrolled.
- Multi-face recognition: every face in a photo is detected, boxed (OpenCV) and compared separately; per-face similarity %, closest registered people, and one suspect profile per identified person with a record (group photos). Video reports each person seen in >= 2 frames. `FaceMatch` model, `Intake.annotated`, migration `cases/0003`. API returns `faces` and `profiles`.
- Validation check `single_subject` replaced by `unique_in_frame`; default `opencv-sface` threshold raised 0.363 -> 0.50 after a false match at 0.386 on a 57-face photo.
- Erasing a person erases every intake showing them (incl. group photos) but keeps other people's profiles.
- 191 tests; the browser flow (webcam photo/video capture, enrol, recognise) was also driven in headless Chrome with a fake camera.
